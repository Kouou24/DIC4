# Spec Delta: paper-conclusion-a2z

## Purpose

Defines the W-to-Z SOP structure and grounding requirements for `V. Conclusion` of the LabSteward ICEIB paper.

## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: W-to-Z Conclusion Structure
`\section{Conclusion}` SHALL follow steps `W` through `Z` of `My_ref/A2Z outline example.pdf`: What proposed (`W`), eXcel scenarios (`X`), Yields summary (`Y`), and Zen & future work (`Z`).

#### Scenario: W-to-Z flow in Conclusion
- **WHEN** `\section{Conclusion}` in `DIC4/main.tex` is reviewed
- **THEN** it recaps the proposed problem/architecture (`W`), highlights scenarios where LabSteward excels (`X`), summarizes grounded expected yields without fabricated metrics (`Y`), and concludes with future work (`Z`).
